Summary & recommendations
How data flows: the robot posts each scan to /api/ingest (pose + list of AP RSSI readings). “Live robot” polls /api/live every 3 s and rebuilds the map from the database as readings arrive. “Survey model” shows the analysed output of the recorded survey (heatmap, contention, clustering, AP localisation, ML benchmark). “Replay survey” streams the recorded readings through the same live pipeline in your browser, for demos without the robot.
